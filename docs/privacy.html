隐私政策postiztest · Privacy Policy

生效及更新日期 / Effective and updated: 2026-10-18
运营者 / Operator: JuHaoLi

当前状态：应用在运营者的本地设备运行，尚未接通 TikTok，也未启用外部 AI 服务。本页同时说明后续启用 TikTok 授权测试时的数据处理方式；不会因访问本网站而自动获取你的 TikTok 数据。

The application runs on the operator's local device. TikTok integration and external AI services are not currently enabled. This policy also explains data handling if TikTok authorization testing is enabled later. Visiting this website does not grant access to your TikTok data.

1. 处理哪些数据 / Data categories

  • 本地应用账号：用于登录的邮箱、工作区资料、密码哈希及会话数据。应用登录密码与 TikTok 密码不同。
  • 素材与排期：参与者主动提供的图片、视频、文案、发布时间、目标渠道，以及任务状态和平台返回的结果。
  • 启用 TikTok 集成后：按实际获准权限和用户授权，处理账号标识（如 open ID）、展示资料（如昵称、头像）、访问与刷新令牌；只有启用且获准相关权限时，才处理作品列表或统计信息。应用不要求运营者收集 TikTok 密码。
  • 技术记录：为运行和排查问题产生的请求、错误与任务日志，可能包含时间、账号或任务标识等信息。

Local application data includes login email, workspace details, password hashes and session data. User-provided media, captions, schedules, destination channels, task status and platform results are processed. If TikTok integration is enabled, authorized data may include account identifiers such as open ID, profile display information and access/refresh tokens; post lists or statistics are processed only when the relevant features and permissions are enabled. The operator does not require TikTok passwords. Operational and diagnostic logs may contain timestamps and account or task identifiers.

2. 用于什么目的 / Purposes

数据仅用于本地测试所需的登录与访问控制、展示已连接账号、管理素材、执行用户确认的上传或发布任务、显示任务结果，以及故障排查和维护。不会出售个人数据，也不将测试素材或账号数据用于广告定向或训练自有 AI 模型。

Data is used for test-related authentication and access control, displaying connected accounts, media management, user-confirmed uploads or publications, task results, troubleshooting and maintenance. Personal data is not sold, and test media or account data is not used for advertising targeting or training the operator's AI models.

3. 向哪些服务传输 / Sharing and third parties

TikTok：启用集成并经用户确认后，应用通过 TikTok 官方接口传输完成请求所需的授权信息、素材、文案和发布设置。已传输或发布的数据还适用 TikTok 的政策。URL 拉取方式需要 TikTok 能访问相应素材；启用此方式前应说明素材可访问范围。

GitHub Pages：本政策网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问时 GitHub 可能按其政策处理 IP 地址、请求信息等技术数据。本站没有嵌入统计、广告、外部字体或第三方脚本，也不设置应用登录 Cookie。本地 Postiz 使用的认证 Cookie 与此静态站点分开。

其他服务：当前没有启用外部 AI 服务，也不会因连接 TikTok 而自动将内容发送给模型提供商。若后续启用 AI、远程存储、代理或隧道等会引入新数据接收方的服务，将先更新相应说明并取得必要授权。依法需要时，数据也可能向有权机关披露。

When enabled and confirmed by the user, the application sends necessary authorization information, media, captions and publishing settings to TikTok through official APIs. TikTok's policies govern its processing. URL-based media transfer requires TikTok to access the media; access exposure should be explained before enabling it. GitHub Pages hosts this policy website and may process IP addresses and request information under its policies. This site embeds no analytics, advertising, remote fonts or third-party scripts and sets no application authentication cookies. Local Postiz authentication cookies are separate. External AI is not enabled, and connecting TikTok does not automatically send content to model providers. New AI, remote storage, proxy or tunnel services involving new data recipients will require updated disclosures and necessary authorization. Disclosures may also be made where legally required.

4. 存储与安全 / Storage and security

当前应用数据保存在运营者本地设备的数据库、上传目录及持久化容器卷中，不存放在本政策网站的公开仓库。应用接口目前只监听本机地址。密钥和授权凭证不应写入公开源码、公开 Issues 或截图。

运营者通过限制设备与配置访问来保护数据；不能保证任何系统绝对安全。本政策不声称数据库或备份已进行字段级或端到端加密。本地设备所在位置与第三方服务处理数据的位置可能不同，跨境处理取决于参与者所在地和服务提供方。

Application data is stored in databases, upload directories and persistent container volumes on the operator's local device, not in this policy website's public repository. The application currently listens on a loopback address only. Secrets and tokens must not be placed in public code, Issues or screenshots. Access to the device and configuration is restricted, but absolute security cannot be guaranteed. This policy does not claim field-level or end-to-end encryption of databases or backups. Third-party processing locations may differ from the local device location, and cross-border processing depends on participant location and provider practices.

5. 保存多久 / Retention

账号、素材、排期和相关令牌保留至测试不再需要、被用户或运营者删除，或授权失效后完成清理。停止或重启 Postiz 不会自动删除持久化数据；撤销 TikTok 授权也不会自动删除本地素材。当前未建立自动到期清理机制。

删除请求应覆盖相应活动数据库记录、素材、待执行任务及授权凭证。日志或已有备份如仍包含相关数据，需一并检查并在技术可行及法律允许的范围内删除或停止恢复使用；不能将断开连接视作已清除所有副本。已在 TikTok 发布的内容需另行处理。

Accounts, media, schedules and related tokens are retained until no longer needed for testing, deleted by the participant/operator, or cleaned up after authorization expires. Stopping or restarting Postiz does not erase persistent data, and revoking TikTok authorization does not delete local media. There is currently no automatic expiry cleanup. Deletion requests should cover relevant active database records, media, pending tasks and credentials. Relevant logs and any existing backups must also be checked and deleted or excluded from restoration where technically and legally feasible. Disconnecting is not equivalent to erasing all copies. Content published on TikTok must be handled separately.

6. 撤销授权、访问与删除 / Revocation and deletion

当前测试仅面向运营者及受邀参与者。参与者可通过邀请测试时已使用的直接联系渠道，向运营者提出访问、更正或删除请求。为避免冒领，处理前可能需要核实请求者对相应本地账号或 TikTok 账号的控制权；不要为此发送密码或访问令牌。

  1. 停止后续访问：在 TikTok 的设置中找到应用授权或已连接应用管理（菜单名称可能因版本而异），撤销 postiztest 的授权；同时在 Postiz 中断开相应渠道并取消待执行任务。
  2. 删除本地数据：要求运营者清理相应账号记录、令牌、素材及任务。本地设备所有者也可自行执行删除；仅停止容器不足以完成删除。
  3. 删除已发布内容：在 TikTok 中管理或删除已发布作品。应用侧删除不保证同步删除平台内容。

如无法使用已有直接联系渠道，可先在公开 Issues中仅留言“请求安排私密的数据处理联系渠道”，不要附上账号标识、邮箱、素材或其他个人信息。运营者应先安排私密方式，再接收和核实具体请求。此政策网站不提供私密表单或自动删除接口。

Testing is limited to the operator and invited participants. Request access, correction or deletion through the direct contact channel used for your test invitation. Control of the relevant account may need to be verified; never send passwords or tokens. Revoke postiztest in TikTok's connected-app permissions, disconnect the Postiz channel and cancel pending tasks. Ask the operator to remove relevant local account records, credentials, media and tasks; stopping containers does not erase them. Manage published content separately on TikTok. If the existing direct channel is unavailable, submit only a generic request for a private privacy-contact channel through public Issues, without account identifiers, email, media or personal data. The operator should arrange a private channel before collecting details. This site has no private request form or automatic deletion endpoint.

This policy will be updated before public access, new permissions or new third-party processing are introduced, with renewed consent where necessary.
